Bringing Reinforcement Learning to Salzburg

Coming from CERN, where I ran the first deep RL experiment on an accelerator, I introduced reinforcement learning into the teaching at the University of Salzburg in 2020: first as a seminar topic, then as dedicated courses, until it became a regular part of the curriculum.

  • WS 2020/21: first RL teaching, Topics in Data Science: Reinforcement Learning (SE 405.970).
  • SS 2021 and SS 2022: first dedicated course, Einführung in Deep Reinforcement Learning (UV 405.986), with CERN guest lectures.
  • SS 2023: Advanced Survey of Reinforcement Learning in the MSc Data Science and the DAS doctoral programme (DSM.105).
  • Since SS 2024: Reinforcement Learning lecture and exercise (VO 536.111 / UE 536.112) in the BSc Artificial Intelligence curriculum, taught every year.
  • SS 2026: Advanced Reinforcement Learning and Agentic AI Systems (DSM.110) in the MSc Data Science, also open to MSc Computer Science and DAS PhD students.
  • Beyond the curriculum: the International RL Bootcamp (since 2024), the monthly RL Coffee, and RL training for companies via DIH West.

Teaching Philosophy

My teaching approach emphasizes the connection between theory and practice. I believe in:

  • Hands-on Learning: Students implement algorithms and work on real-world problems
  • Interdisciplinary Thinking: Connecting RL concepts to physics, control theory, and domain applications
  • Research Integration: Exposing students to cutting-edge research and open problems
  • Industry Relevance: Bridging academic concepts with practical industrial applications
